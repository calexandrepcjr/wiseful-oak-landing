# Welcome to the Wiseful Oak Newsroom

We're opening a window into how we work. This space will feature short, candid notes from the field — patterns we see across embedded engagements, decisions we've made, and lessons that survived contact with reality.

## What you'll find here

- **Field notes** from active engagements (sanitized, of course)
- **Architecture decisions** and the trade-offs behind them
- **Cost reduction case studies** with concrete before/after numbers
- **Hiring and team philosophy** as we grow

## Why now

After two years operating with U.S. clients through our LLC, we've accumulated enough recurring patterns that writing them down feels overdue. The goal isn't thought leadership theatre — it's a working journal we'd want to read ourselves.

> Clients should never feel dependent on us — only strengthened by the time we worked together.
